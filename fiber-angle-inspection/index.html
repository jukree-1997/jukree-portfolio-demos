BEHIND THE PROJECT / AUG–SEP 2026

From a manual QC step
to a connected workflow.

The customer wanted to photograph fibers, measure their recovery angles, and retrieve job information automatically from SQL Server.

01 / CUSTOMER DISCOVERY

Understand the process first.

I met the customer, gathered requirements, and translated their QC workflow into an application and measurement approach.

02 / INDEPENDENT DEVELOPMENT

Both sides of the solution.

I built the UI and measurement workflow myself. OpenCV supported fixture registration and fiber geometry, while the web app connected job metadata, inspection records, corrections and reports.

03 / BUSINESS VALUE

Demonstrate a practical alternative.

The demo showed how image-based inspection could combine measurements with job records and reduce repeated manual measurement and data entry. It was adapted from a development PC to a mini PC.

ORIGINAL APPLICATION

Customer data meets computer vision.

React/TypeScript provides the operator interface. FastAPI coordinates SQL Server job metadata and a classical OpenCV pipeline with ArUco fixture registration, per-slot preprocessing, candidate line fitting and geometric checks.

What is working here, and what belongs to the original application?

Working here: fictional job selection, real angle mathematics on synthetic vectors, example tolerance checks, mixed-result handling, immutable in-memory batch snapshots, linked corrections, CSV/JSON downloads and browser PDF printing.

Not executed: camera acquisition, OpenCV detection, ArUco decoding, image-based calibration, MSSQL queries, durable storage or server-generated PDF/XLSX reports. This is a workflow demonstration, not a validated inspection instrument.
